Common questions

My pet does not appear on the screen.

Open Android settings, Apps, Shimeji On Screen, and turn on "Display over other apps". Some phones also need battery optimisation turned off for the app so Android does not stop the pets in the background.

How do I stop the pets?

Open the app and tap stop, or use the notification that is shown while pets are running.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you subscribed with, open Shimeji On Screen, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Shimeji On Screen, Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the app's settings and tap Privacy options.
