Privacy Policy for Shimeji On Screen

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Shimeji On Screen (shown on Google Play as Desktop Pet: Shimeji On Screen) is an Android app that shows cute animated pets walking and climbing on top of your phone screen. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app with package com.desktoppet.shimeji.onscreen, published on Google Play by Billionweb.

The short version

  • The app never reads what is on your screen. The "Display over other apps" permission is used only to draw your pets on top of other apps. The app cannot see, record or capture the content of other apps.
  • There is no account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads in the free version. These receive device and usage information described below.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised or your data is used for advertising, and you can change that choice at any time.

Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Display over other appsTo draw your pets on top of your home screen and other apps. Your pets are pictures drawn by the app; nothing from other apps is read or saved.
Notifications and foreground serviceAndroid requires a visible notification while pets are running on your screen, so you always know the app is active and can stop it.
Internet and network stateTo load ads, send analytics and crash reports, and check your subscription with Google Play.

You can turn off "Display over other apps" in Android settings at any time. Your pets then stop appearing outside the app.

Information that stays on your device

The pets you choose, how many are on screen, their size and speed, and your other settings are stored only on your device.

These services are provided by Google and process data under Google's own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising and your consent choices

The free version shows ads. Premium shows none. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether your data may be used for advertising and measurement. Until you choose, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's settings under Privacy options. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Privacy, Ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a weekly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We receive no card details, no billing address and no name. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms as described. These providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards.

Retention

Your settings remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data follows Google's retention practices.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, or to exercise any other privacy right you have under local law, email arshpreet@billionweb.co with the subject "Shimeji On Screen data deletion". Because there is no account, we will explain what we can locate and what limited information, if any, we need. Never send passwords or identity documents.

Children

Shimeji On Screen is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.
